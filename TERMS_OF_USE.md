## 2. Description of the App

Chamfer is a viewer for CAD drawings that lets you:

- Keep DWG, DXF, DWF, DWFx, XPS and STL files in a library on your device and sort them into folders
- Open drawings, zoom and pan them, and switch between their layouts
- Show and hide layers
- Export drawings to PDF and print them

The App shows drawings. It does not edit them.
